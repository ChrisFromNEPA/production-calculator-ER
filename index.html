⚡ Advanced slot setup
Set in levels, the way they actually get talked about — most production runs at 5 energy, 0 cooling. Energy can't go below 1; cooling can be left off entirely. Each energy level adds about 1.43 UC per run and each cooling level 0.95, flat regardless of what you're making — so at the top it adds 50 UC whether the run costs 69 or 4997, dominating a cheap material and barely registering on a gun. Cooling isn't dead weight though: it keeps heat down, and heat is what drags effectiveness and speed with it. These totals are the price of a cold start; a long run drifts cheaper per unit. Defense Rating = chance to reduce an attack of any type · Block Rating = chance to negate one entirely · Protection Reduction = debuff cutting all five protections at once.

Regen floors (nothing equipped): HP 2.0% · Stam 2.0% · Bio 0.0% · Aura 2.5% · Addiction Treatment 0.0% · Medkit CD 0.0s. Health/stam/aura regen are free; bio must be fed (bio cells, injectors, illegal substances).

Drains are the upkeep cost of gear/boosters — 0 with nothing on. Every regen source list includes illegal substances, and addiction only clears at the slow natural rate without Addiction Treatment (BoS's specialty).

⛏️ How a slot works — energy, cooling, heat and cost

Cost of one batch

Start from the item's listed price, add the slot upkeep, then the colony's tax. Mining triples the base before any of that, which is already baked into the material prices shown elsewhere in the app.

  • Energy runs 1–21 and cooling 0–21. Energy can't be switched off; cooling can. Most production sits at 5 energy, 0 cooling.
  • Both raise the price, by a flat amount per level: 1.43 UC per energy level and 0.95 per cooling level.
  • Tax is not a plain markup. A rate above 10% discounts the base first.

Heat and speed

Energy generates heat continuously and cooling removes it. As heat rises, effectiveness falls and production slows. A long hot run is slower but cheaper per unit.

Still not modelled: exact speed loss. This page estimates cost, not run duration.
